Riccardo Conforti

@Nfoedy

From the Marche region of Italy, with a passion for video games and the ambition to become a Multiplayer/Networking Programmer. My learning path, including the Master in Computer Game Development, focuses on development with Unreal Engine 5 and C++, complemented by experience with Unity and C#. I am particularly interested in the technical challenges of Multiplayer and Networking and in designing modular, reusable gameplay systems. I enjoy understanding how they work in depth, tackling technical challenges and finding robust, extensible solutions. I value teamwork, constructive discussion and environments that encourage me to challenge myself. My goal is to keep growing professionally and contribute to multiplayer experiences across different genres.
